# Audit, October 2026: the site, its data, and what was fixed

Scope: the web app (`app/`), the data it reads (`app/data/`), its API routes and tests, and
the model outputs it presents. The model engine itself was audited in earlier rounds
(`docs/TOP500K_PLAN.md`); engine findings here are the ones the site exposed.

## The new site

Seven tabs, each a URL hash so they can be bookmarked:

- **This week**: the model's team for the next deadline, laid out on a pitch with captain, vice and bench order; its transfers with six-gameweek gains; every chip's value against the bar it must clear; injury news for the squad.
- **Players**: all players with search, position, team, price, starter and fitness filters; sortable columns (xPts, six-GW xPts, points per £m, start, 5+ and 8+ chances, price-change chances); presets for best next GW, best over six, value, differentials and price risers/fallers; tick up to four to compare.
- **Captain**: the top fifteen options with haul and blank chances, optionally only from your team.
- **Fixtures**: the next three to six gameweeks for every club from the live FPL fixture list, coloured by FPL's difficulty or by the model's own ratings for attackers or defenders, sorted by easiest run; blanks and doubles handled; your clubs highlighted.
- **My team**: import by team ID; rank, value, bank and estimated free transfers; points per gameweek; chips left this half; problem players; the best XI from your squad; suggested transfers; and a replacement finder that respects your bank and the three-per-club rule.
- **Wildcard planner**: the exact squad optimiser in the browser with your budget, must-include and leave-out players, for a six-gameweek Wildcard or a one-week Free Hit.
- **Track record**: the live pick log against its projections, and the eight replayed seasons against the top-500k line.

Every player name opens a detail panel: where the points come from, minutes, outcome chances, track record, team and fixture context, set pieces, price movement and the most-owned alternative.

## Engine and data findings (not fixed here)

- **A. The per-player P10/P90 is not a quantile.** `prediction_p10/p90` are `component_xpts ± prediction_half_width_80`, a symmetric band sized from absolute residuals. FPL points are heavily skewed, so for premium attackers the band misses most outcomes (Haaland: 4.1–7.9 with a 44% blank chance). Derive the interval from the event probabilities or the scenario simulation, or stop publishing it.
- **B. `chipStrategy.current` is stale after every refresh.** `refresh_current_artifact` updates the headline but not this block. Remove it or refresh it there.
- **C. The pick log has no GW4 or GW5 entries.** The scheduled task was not yet recording those weeks.
- **D. The research artifacts are frozen.** `model-audit.json`, `shadow-status.json`, `chip-scenarios.json`, `deadline-status.json` and related files were last written on 29 August and describe an older model. They are no longer shown; delete them with `/api/research` when nothing else needs them.
- **E. Tooling.** The app needs Node 22.13 or later and this machine has Node 6 (bundled with Brackets). A verified portable Node 22.23.3 now sits in `work/tools/` (git-ignored) for building and testing.
